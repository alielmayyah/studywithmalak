# Functional requirements

- Ali and Malak enter one shared room with their own passcodes, without email sign-in.
- Online Presence is separate from a persistent focus, break, eating, away, or done status.
- A personal Pomodoro offers 25/5, 50/10, and custom durations, with pause, resume, break, and end controls. Focus completion starts a break automatically.
- Focus time excludes pauses. Together time counts only overlapping focus intervals, clipped to the local day.
- The room and paged history fit the viewport without page scrolling on desktop and mobile.
- A hidden or closing tab sends a Presence leave; a visible tab rejoins. Connection failures are shown and state reloads after reconnection.
